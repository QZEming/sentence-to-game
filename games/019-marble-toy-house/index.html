WELCOME, LITTLE EXPLORER

Good things are a roll away.

Use WASD or arrow keys to roll your marble through the toy house. Press Space to hop over obstacles, and Shift to brake.

Collect the 12 golden stars, touch the mint checkpoint flags, then reach the glowing garden portal. Orange strips give you a boost. Watch out for spinning toy windmills!

C returns to your checkpoint. R restarts, Esc pauses. Switch to Time trial for a 90-second challenge. On touch screens, use the on-screen arrows.
